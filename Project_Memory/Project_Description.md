## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

### The Challenge
Conventional government procurement frameworks are designed for standardized goods and established vendors. They enforce strict historical turnover cutoffs, multi-year experience requirements, and OEM-locked technical specifications. These barriers prevent innovative startups from participating in public tenders, while government departments miss out on modern, agile, and cost-effective solutions to pressing operational challenges.

### The Solution Proposed with the Expected Solution: Project Sahyog
**Project Sahyog** is a centralized, AI-driven Gov-Tech platform designed to bridge the gap between government departments and DPIIT-recognized startups. It provides an end-to-end pathway—from problem statement drafting and blind evaluation to risk-free sandbox piloting, SLA-tracked escrow payments, and direct national scale-up on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM).

## 3. End-to-End Operational Workflow

1. **Discover & Match**: Government departments log in to draft problem statements. The NLP engine screens for restrictive clauses and suggests outcome-based rewrites.
2. **Screen & Score**: Startups apply via 1-click DPIIT sync. The rule-based capability engine evaluates startups on technical capability rather than turnover.
3. **Blind Evaluation**: Evaluators score anonymized submissions on a weighted scorecard within a locked environment.
4. **Pilot & Certify**: Shortlisted startups enter a risk-free Sandbox testbed under the Safe-Harbour Shield.
5. **Validate & Fund**: Independent 3rd-party evaluators submit KPI reports. Upon validation, the escrow system auto-releases milestone payments within 15 days.
6. **Scale & Rollout**: Proven solutions receive a Multi-State Pilot Passport and transition seamlessly to GeM for direct public procurement.
